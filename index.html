Workflow Blueprint

Detailed appointment workflow

Built directly from the handwritten blueprint PDF.

1

Patient books appointment

Patient books an online appointment through the website. The appointment information is transferred from the Website to the Admin App.

2

Admin app receives notification

The MMH admin receives a notification about the new appointment. The appointment appears inside the app dashboard.

3

Appointment dashboard

The app shows Appointment Information, Appointment Status, and Patient Contact.

  • Status options: New, Confirm, Dismiss, Visit Done
  • Admin can inspect each appointment record
4

Contact patient

Admin taps the Patient Contact button. The calling app opens automatically with the patient’s registered phone number.

5

Fee collection and WhatsApp sharing

Admin confirms the appointment during the call and collects the registration fee through WhatsApp/payment link. Patient information can also be shared through WhatsApp.

6

Receptionist creates ticket

The receptionist uses the shared patient information to create a ticket. A unique USSID code is generated for the patient.

7

Confirm appointment

When Confirm is clicked, a dialog asks for USSID, date of visit, and time of visit. After fill-up, the appointment becomes confirmed.

8

Patient receives notification

The registered phone number and email receive a confirmation message with USSID, date, and time of visit.
